Your rights under GDPR (Art. 15–21)

You can request a copy of all personal data we hold about you at any time.

You can ask us to delete all your data. We will do so within 30 days unless a legal retention obligation applies.
Right to rectification
If any data we hold is incorrect, you can ask us to correct it.
Right to withdraw consent
You can withdraw your consent at any time. This does not affect the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal.
Right to data portability
You can ask for your data in a machine-readable format (CSV).

You can object to processing based on legitimate interest at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, email us at info@animmobilien.de. We will respond within 30 days.
