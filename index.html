02 Phase two

Evaluate

Before you make an offer, know exactly what the property is worth and what risks you're taking on.

Price evaluation €199
"Is this property worth what they're asking?"
Essential +

Never make an offer blind. We analyse comparable sales, market trends, and property condition to give you a fair value range and a recommended offer price — with a walk-away ceiling.

  • Comparable sales analysis (3–5 recent properties)
  • Price per m² benchmark against the postcode
  • Condition adjustment for renovation needs
  • Hausgeld and hidden cost check for apartments
  • Fair value range and recommended offer price
  • One-page written report with your verdict

Financing assessment €199
"Can I actually get a mortgage for this?"
Optional +

Before going deep on due diligence, make sure you can finance it. We assess your mortgage readiness and connect you with trusted English-speaking brokers who understand the Indian expat profile.

  • Review of your income, savings, and financial profile
  • Estimate of borrowing capacity based on German criteria
  • Introduction to 2–3 vetted English-speaking mortgage brokers
  • Guidance on documents German banks typically require
  • Clarity on what to expect from the application process
Book Financing Assessment →
Due diligence €499
"Are there any hidden risks I should know about?"
Essential +

Do this when you're serious about a property. We go through every document, flag every risk, and give you a clear go / no-go before you commit to anything. One property, one thorough report.

  • Full Grundbuch (land registry) review
  • Teilungserklärung and Eigentümergemeinschaft documents for apartments
  • Check for outstanding debts, liens, or encumbrances
  • Review of meeting minutes for upcoming special assessments
  • Energy certificate and building permit checks
  • Written risk summary with clear go / no-go recommendation
Book Due Diligence →
Renovation assessment €299
"What will it cost to make this liveable?"
Optional +

Know the real cost before you commit. We identify what needs doing, what can wait, and give you a realistic budget — so there are no surprises after you've signed.

  • Identification of immediate vs. future work
  • Must-have, should-have, nice-to-have priority list
  • Budget estimate by category (kitchen, bathroom, floors, etc.)
  • Rough timeline for works
  • Connections to trusted contractors if needed
